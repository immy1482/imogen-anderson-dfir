### Key takeaway ⋆‧°𓏲ּ𝄢
What is the most important thing I learned this week?
Accurate documentation and maintaining the integrity of digital evidence are essential in digital forensics. Every action must be recorded clearly enough for another examiner to understand and replicate the process, helping ensure that evidence can be relied upon during an investigation or court proceedings.
